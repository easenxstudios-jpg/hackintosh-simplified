This is an early preview.

The scan has run on one machine. Boot fixes haven't been tried on a real EFI, and a bad boot change can stop your Mac starting. Keep a bootable USB with a working OpenCore. Kexts come from each project's own releases, and the app can't confirm they're safe or suit your macOS version.

Three steps to start.

  1. DownloadOpen the .dmg and drag the app to Applications. It needs an Intel processor and macOS 13 or later.
  2. OpenmacOS blocks it the first time, because it isn't notarized. On macOS 15 or later, click Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. On 13 or 14, right-click the app and choose Open.
  3. ScanThe first scan starts by itself. Before applying any fix, read what it will change.
