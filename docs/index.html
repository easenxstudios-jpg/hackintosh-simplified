Hackintosh Simplified

A free, open-source Mac app that checks your hackintosh, tells you what's wrong in plain words, and helps fix what it safely can.

Download for Intel Macs View source on GitHub

Early preview. Use it at your own risk. The scan has run on one machine. Boot fixes haven't been tried on a real EFI, and a bad boot change can stop your Mac starting. Keep a bootable USB with a working OpenCore.

What a scan looks like

WorkingSound, USB 3 at 5 Gb/s, Metal graphics
Needs attentionEthernet limited to 100 Mb/s
Needs attentionExperimental Realtek Wi-Fi driver
ProblemAirDrop: no awdl0 link, no software fix known

What it does

  • Scans without changing anything. It needs no password and doesn't read your serial number.
  • Shows every change before applying a fix, and backs up your whole EFI before boot changes.
  • Guides you through jobs that depend on your machine, like USB maps and Wi-Fi drivers.
  • Says when it has no fix, instead of offering one that won't work.

Install

  1. Download the .dmg. It needs an Intel processor and macOS 13 or later.
  2. Open it and drag the app to Applications.
  3. Open the app. macOS blocks it the first time, because it isn't notarized.
  4. On macOS 15 or later, click Open Anyway in System Settings > Privacy & Security. On macOS 13 or 14, right-click the app and choose Open.
